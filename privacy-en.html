Privacy Policy

ChenJiTang · Professional Client Management System for Astrology Practitioners
Effective date: August 1, 2026
Last updated: September 19, 2026
Scope: The "ChenJiTang" iOS application (the "App") and related services

Thank you for using ChenJiTang. We recognize that astrological data is highly personal, so the App was designed around the principle of keeping data on your device whenever possible. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and what rights you have.

1. What Data We Collect

1.1 Data You Provide

Data typeContentStorage location
Account data Email address, display name Cloud (Firebase)
Case records Name, gender, date/time of birth, place of birth, marital status, occupation, personal notes and life events Stored locally on your device only
AI consultation content The questions you enter, and the topics/scenarios you select Sent to our server for processing; the response is stored locally on your device
Important: Chart data you create (including sensitive information such as birth time) is stored only on your phone by default and is not automatically uploaded to the cloud. Only when you actively use the "AI Consultation" feature is the chart data needed for that specific request sent to our server for processing.

1.2 Data Collected Automatically

  • User ID (UID): Automatically generated by Firebase upon registration, used to identify your account and entitlements.
  • Device identifier: Apple's "identifierForVendor" and an App-generated local device ID, used to prevent abuse of the Service. For subscribed accounts, this is also used to enforce the "one phone and one tablet" sign-in limit described below. This identifier becomes invalid once you delete the App.
  • Usage analytics: We use Firebase Analytics to record events such as sign-in, sign-up, purchases, AI feature usage, and case-record saves, along with their associated feature names, to understand overall usage and improve the product. These events never include the content of your case records, your AI questions, or the full text of AI responses.
  • Service usage logs: Number of AI consultations, topic categories, computational load, and timestamps. Does not include your questions or the full text of AI responses.
  • Purchase records: Apple transaction ID, product code, and purchase time, used to grant and restore your entitlements.

1.3 What We Do Not Collect

  • The App does not use any third-party advertising or behavioral-tracking SDKs, and is not used to serve ads.
  • We do not collect your contacts, precise location, health data, or photo-library contents (camera/photo-library access is used only momentarily when you actively scan or save a QR code, and no copy is retained).
  • Face ID / Touch ID biometric data is managed entirely by iOS and is never sent to us or leaves your device.
  • We never store your password in plain text; passwords are managed in encrypted form by Firebase Authentication.

2. How We Use This Data

  • Providing the Service: chart calculation, AI astrological analysis, and storing your purchased entitlements.
  • Account management: identity verification, sending email-verification and password-reset messages.
  • Transaction processing: confirming Apple payment results and granting the corresponding entitlements, and handling "Restore Purchases" requests.
  • Abuse prevention: the subscription "one phone and one tablet" sign-in protection, daily usage-quota controls, and preventing duplicate entitlement grants.
  • Service operations and product improvement: using aggregate usage analytics to plan system capacity and cost, and to understand how features are used.

We never use your personal data for advertising, and we never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party.

We may also disclose your data in the following circumstances:

  • In response to a court order, legal requirement, or request from a competent authority.
  • Where necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of the Service, our users, or third parties.
  • With your prior explicit consent.

4. Data Retention

  • Account and entitlement data: retained until you delete your account.
  • Consultation session logs: only the number of interactions and timestamps are kept; conversation content is not retained.
  • Usage and transaction records: retained for 5 years for accounting and tax purposes.
  • Local case data: entirely under your control; deleting the App removes it as well.

5. Security Measures

  • All network traffic is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.
  • Case data is encrypted on-device using iOS's file-protection mechanisms.
  • Exported backup files are encrypted with AES-GCM and require a password you set yourself to open; we have no way to recover this password if lost, so please keep it safe.
  • Changes to entitlements and credits can only be executed server-side, preventing tampering.
  • Sign-in credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain.

6. Your Rights

Under the Personal Data Protection Act of Taiwan, the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance of Hong Kong, the Personal Data Protection Act of Macau, Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA), and Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA), you have the following rights over your personal data:

  • Access: view your account data and entitlement status in the App's "Member Center."
  • Correction: your display name can be changed at any time in the "Member Center."
  • Deletion: select "Delete Account" in the "Member Center" to permanently remove your account and cloud data. This action is irreversible.
  • Data portability: use the "Export Backup" feature in the App to obtain a copy of your case data.
  • Withdrawal: you may stop using the Service and delete your account at any time.

To exercise any of these rights or if you have any questions, please contact us using the details below.

7. Children's Privacy

This Service is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. Minors must obtain the consent of a parent or legal guardian before using this Service. If you become aware that a minor has provided us with personal data without such consent, please contact us immediately and we will delete it promptly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this policy from time to time due to regulatory changes or service updates. The "Last updated" date on this page will be revised accordingly; material changes will be announced prominently within the App.
