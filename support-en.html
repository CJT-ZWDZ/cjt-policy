Frequently Asked Questions

I made a purchase but didn't receive my entitlement. What should I do?

First, confirm you have a stable internet connection and tap the refresh icon in the top-right corner of "Member Center." If it still hasn't arrived, tap "Restore Purchases" on the purchase page. If the issue persists, please email us with your purchase time and the product name, and we'll help resolve it.

How do I request a refund?

All purchases are processed through the Apple App Store, and refunds must be requested directly from Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, select the transaction, and submit your request.

Could I lose my chart data?

Case data is stored primarily on your device. We recommend regularly using "Export Encrypted Backup" to avoid data loss when switching devices or in case of accidental deletion. The backup file requires the password you set to open it, so please keep it safe.

How accurate is the AI analysis?

AI-assisted analysis offers "Daily" (daily star transformation analysis) and "In-Depth Report" modes as supplementary reference tools for astrological consultations. The content is AI-generated and may not be entirely accurate — it's for reference only. Astrological interpretation varies across schools of thought, so conclusions from different sources may differ. Please do not treat it as the sole basis for major medical, legal, or financial decisions.

I got a new phone — are my purchased entitlements still there?

Yes. Sign in with the same account on your new device, then tap "Restore Purchases" on the AI or upgrade page to sync your purchased entitlements.

Note: If you're a subscriber, this Service allows only one phone and one tablet to be signed in at a time. Signing in on a new phone will automatically sign out the previously signed-in phone, with an on-screen notice — this protects your subscription from being shared with others. Tablet sign-ins are unaffected. Free/unsubscribed accounts are not subject to this limit.

How do I delete my account and data?

Scroll to the bottom of "Member Center" and select "Delete Account" to permanently remove your account and cloud data. This action cannot be undone, so please back up any case data you want to keep beforehand.
